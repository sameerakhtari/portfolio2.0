## Rendering boundaries

DOM owns all text, navigation, statuses and controls. SVG owns accurate diagrams and trace topology. A reusable WebGL pigment compositor turns original illustrations into sketches and reveals color with a multi-source noisy arrival field. Canvas2D supplies an equivalent lower-resolution fallback. Three.js owns dimensional compute/hardware diagrams, with the same arrival field embedded in material shaders. No paragraphs or essential navigation live inside GPU surfaces.

## Motion and performance

Scroll is damped, not intercepted. Intersection observers suspend inactive scenes; GPU pixel ratio is capped. The 3D module is loaded near its first visible scene. Materials, listeners, observers and renderers are disposed on unmount. Reduced motion presents composed, colored diagrams in normal document flow. A user motion control is retained locally. Image and WebGL failures retain readable semantic diagrams and alternate content.
